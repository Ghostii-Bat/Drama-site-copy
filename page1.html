Mean Girls! (Nov. 2026)

Mean Girls is a musical that explores the harshest parts of High School! It's an exaggeration of an everyday high school experience dealing with tough themes such as hate, suicide and bullying.

The musical depicts the character, Cady Heron, a 16 year old girl who was homeschooled in Africa before moving to Illinois and joining a public High School.

Not only being a new girl but also new to the concept of a public High School, Cady meets nice friends but falls deep into toxic social circles after meeting “The Plastics” a group of girls who participate in gossip, bullying and being the popular queens of the school.

Regina George is the leader of the plastics, and is known to be a ruthless gossip. She has destroyed the reputations of: Teachers, Students and Administrators all while maintaining her weight of 115 lbs.
