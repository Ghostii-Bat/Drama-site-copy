Club Info, and Club History!

The Drama Club is a long running club spanning to the start of the Opening of SWHS back in 1961, from its humble beginnings in the school cafeteria to the upgraded building including the Auditorium– This Club has seen plenty of South Windsor students perform on stage.

The history of theater at South Windsor High School (SWHS) is a continuity of artistic growth and community that span several decades. During the late 20th century, the drama club solidified its presence under the leadership of impactful student groups and dedicated instructors. By the late 1990s and early 2000s, the program had earned a reputation for high-quality productions. It served as a vital training ground for remarkable alumni, including SWHS Hall of Fame inductees like opera singer Kristen Plumley (Class of 1983) and Kyle Anderson (Class of 2000), who starred in lead turns for foundational shows like Camelot, The Sound of Music, and Bye Bye Birdie. Hollywood actor and comedian Brent Morin also sharpened his performance skills within the walls of the SWHS theater department.

The program entered recent decades like the 2010s and the 2020s, although seeing a significant dip in performances in 2020 (COVID-19). The Drama department significantly increased its production value by offering diverse, demanding and challenging scores.Thanks to Directors like, Caroline Schroer, Mrs. White, and Bryn Chandler, the club consistently expanded its creative boundaries, successfully tackling contemporary, complex pieces such as The Addams Family, Into the Woods, Legally Blonde, Radium Girls, and Once Upon a Mattress, as well as expanding into self-produced production like in Unified Theatre!

The Unified Theatre is a club allied with The SWHS Drama Club, students in this club create their own script, song-list and work together to create a production at the end of the year! The club is a great way to get involved in the community!, especially for freshman, contact Terri White (Mrs.White), to get more information on the club!!

In the present day, the Drama Department continues to push out memorable plays and musicals, integrating modern technologies and fresh performances to inspire young actors! This forward-thinking approach is evident in its lineup of fast-paced comedies and beloved contemporary hits, including recent and upcoming school productions of the Harry Potter-inspired Puffs and the modern pop-culture favorite Mean Girls. Supported by passionate faculty advisors like Ms. Correale, the drama club remains a staple of the local arts scene, drawing large audiences from the South Windsor community and fostering a vibrant creative home for future generations of theater artists.
