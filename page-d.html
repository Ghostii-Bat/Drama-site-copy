Legally Blonde (Nov. 2024)

The SWHS Theater Department performed Legally Blonde: The Musical during November of 2024. While preparing for the show was a tough task, and time consuming for all students involved, their hard work paid off, as the show was a success. All 3 showings of the musical were packed with the audience eager to see the performance, many reporting that they were extremely impressed with the set design, and others deeply appreciated the musical talent from the students performing. To learn more about our performers, the playbill will provide you with information.

3 students from this production were also nominated for the 2025 Stephen Sondheim Awards.

Nominees

  • Tiernan Cabot - Best Actor
  • Rowan Pott - Best Supporting Actress
  • Aditi Varma - Best Costume Design

    • It was truly an honor and great recognition for SWHS to be nominated.

Overall, this musical was one of SWHS Theater Department’s best productions in recent years, with the set design, costumes, and some of the best acting/singing yet.
