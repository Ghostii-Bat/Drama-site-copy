The Play That Goes Wrong (2024)

The play for the 2023- 2024 school year was The Play That Goes Wrong. The play follows an amateur theater troop as they try to put on a performance of the Murder of Hammersham Manor but are interrupted when theater hijinks ensue. From set mishaps to forgotten lines to actors being knocked out, in the end you’ll be left wondering will the play ever go right?

The production in SWHS was double casted, meaning that there were two actors for each role. This gave us a better opportunity to show off our skills and bolstered a different experience depending on the cast. The set design was also very complicated as you had to somehow create a set that looked like it was falling apart for the sake of the show, but also be able to reset everything. Our set designer created a set that was designed with removable bolts, so set pieces could fall apart when needed. Actors, when not acting played there part as the crew, the key players in making the play go “wrong”

I (the writer for this piece) acted in the play. I played Annie, the troop's stage manager turned actor when an actor gets knocked out by a door. Annie is thrust into the role of “Florence” in the play, the resident damsel in distress. My favorite part of the play was when The original actor and I finally meet and fight it out for the role. Yes literally fighting, we were acting out punching, hitting, strangling, and body slamming. And the best moment of course was when I won hehe.

By Joann Varghese (Guest Writer/Actor)

Into the Woods (Winter 2023)

The 2023 Winter Musical production of Into the Woods was directed by Bryn Chandler. It follows the fairy tales of Little Red Riding Hood, Jack and the Beanstalk, Rapunzel, and Cinderella as they venture into the woods to get their wishes. But when a scheming Witch makes a deal with her neighbors, the Baker and his Wife, these tales must intertwine to fulfill a spell and lift a curse. When changing the ending of the story, who keeps their happily ever after?

This was my first show in South Windsor High School when I was just a little freshman. Now going into my senior year, I’m one of the only remaining cast members. This show was a wonder to watch come together as my first experience with SWHS Drama! The students were all such incredible singers and actors who were very welcoming towards new members such as myself. I remember that the set pieces had a lot of work put into them. Each fairy tale had its own story book, these open triangles on wheels. They were massive props that were hard to navigate whether on stage or in the wings. There was also a house front for Little Red’s Grandmother’s house suspended in our theater’s fly system that the crew would winch down. Not to mention Rapunzel’s tower in the stage right landing! In addition to our two night shows, our Saturday matinee was specially advertised as “The King’s Festival” for a kid-friendly version of the performance. Rather than both acts, there were activities in the lobby of the school and a performance of only Act 1 of the show so that the children weren’t traumatized by the giants. With all the set pieces, similar sounding (but different!) songs, and altercations to the show it was a lot to put together, especially as the first one I stepped into. But I always wanted to be in a production of Into the Woods and with the determination of the theater team, it turned out happily ever after.

As I said, this was my first show, so of course I didn’t have a big role, but I didn’t ‘just get ensemble’ either. I had the honor of playing Milky White, Jack’s cow that he trades for some old beans. It helped me understand the physical aspects of acting and how to perform with physical comedy as I was given a cow puppet to portray the character. I had so much fun making Milky White Jack’s loyal companion and letting her give some comedic relief to an otherwise dark show. It was so wonderful to see such a positive audience reaction to the show itself along with my personal performance being highlighted.

~Written by Meghan Sands - actor for Milky White in SWHS’s Into the Woods
