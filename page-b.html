Once Upon a Mattress

The South Windsor High School 2025 fall musical was the humorous, high-energy Once Upon A Mattress! Directed by Bryn Chandler and choreographed by Caroline Schroer, this production was a comedic retelling of the famous classic we all know; Princess and the Pea. The kingdom in which is show in based in ruled by King Sextimus the Silent, and Queen Aggravin (who talks enough for the both of them). The entire plot was to find a wife for their child-like son, Dauntless the Drab. Eventually, the thirteenth contestant, Winifred (or, Fred) the Woebegone arrives. Once Upon A Mattress featured amazing actors, singers, and dancers, who made the show such an amazing experience. Energetic dance numbers, namely the Spanish Panic, surely felt like workouts during rehearsals (especially when we had to sing and dance while lugging mattresses on and off stage), but it was so worth it in the end! In fact, this production earned three nomination for various Halo Awards (see section below).

HALO award Nominees

  • Bryce Chandler as Sir Harry for Best Performance in a Featured Male Role in a Musical
  • Nikshita Sree Ganseh Kumar & Lana Kim for Best Program Art
  • Hailey Olints as Minstrel for Best Performance in a Supporting Male Role in a Musical

Since this way my first show with the SWHS drama club, it was a really fun introduction to the group! Everyone was super helpful, and all three performances went really well. The set was honestly one of my favorite elements of the show. We had built a set that resembled a castle, with a turret and a platform (which was very fun to dance on!). There was even a spinning wall that revealed the bed – twenty mattresses high, of course. Personally, I really loved the castle “wall” that Fred fell off of. Behind in there was a pile of mattresses to break the fall. It was really one of those “theater magic” moments where Fred just disappeared off the wall! Overall, it was truly such a fun show.
