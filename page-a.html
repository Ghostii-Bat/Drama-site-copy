Puffs! (Spring 2026)

Copy of Puffs Playbill by Nikshita

Playbill for school chromebooks

Show Overview!

For the 2025-2026 Spring Play, South Windsor High School performed Puffs directed by Caroline Schroer. Puffs is a show set in a certain Wizard School that a certain boy with a scar on his forehead attended. But it’s not about him! It’s about the Puffs, the little guys just doing their best in this magical world with main character adventures happening right next door. It was such an incredibly fun show to do. Personally, I (the writer of this blurb) think it was my favorite show so far! There was a very positive energy around building the show since it was a comedy, and with improv being an element embedded into the script, everyone brought their own spin to things. For example, our actor for the character Zach Smith came up with her own monologue for the character and performed it in two out of our three shows. We also spent rehearsals working on the set and props so each drama club member could have a hand in the visual look of the show. There’s no doubt that this positive vibe is what propelled us to be nominated for nine Halo Awards (and win two).

Our production of Puffs was nominated for:

  • Lana Kim as Mr. Voldy for Best Performance in a Comic Male Role in a Play
  • Adeline Curylo for Best Lighting Design and/or Execution
  • Maddie Edwards & Blaze Hartley for Best Sound Design and/or Execution
  • Luke Marotto for Best Scenic Design and/or Execution
  • All Cast & Crew for Best Props Design
  • Run Crew for Best Running Crew
  • Best Performance by a Cast in an Ensemble Production
  • Claire Correale as Leanne/Ginny/Frenchy/Ric Gryf for Best Standout Performance in a Female Role in an Ensemble Production
  • Best Contemporary Play

Show Insights!

Since there was an improvisational element to the show where characters had different options of dialogue, every show was different. Zach Smith talked about Deborah or Goyle, Mr. Voldy either did Pilates or went barefoot and I switched between three different lines roasting Ron (top choice: “You clearly never got bullied enough”). We got more comfortable with the characters and backstage shenanigans as the show went on so that last show–I’ll admit–we broke character half the time. The comedy was simply too humorous. I think our second show was the best performance since it was also the show being judged for the Halo Awards. Everyone did their best to stay in character and be the funniest they could for the judges which clearly worked in our favor. There were a lot of props and costume changes to keep track of backstage, so it was a good challenge for us to know when our robes needed to be on or off and if this was a Cedric pin scene. It also allowed us to have plenty of keepsakes after set strike. One big thing I remember was when the Show Bingo finally got completed during the last show, after Zach Smith told our inside joke, “Maybe a turtle.” Everyone broke character after that from celebrating. It was all around a wonderful show and I can’t wait to be in the next production. As always, ride that centaur.

We won awards for:

  • Lana Kim as Mr. Voldy for Best Performance in a Comic Male Role in a Play
  • All Cast & Crew for Best Props Design
